# 03 — Water

## Subtopics
- `sourcing/` — finding groundwater, rainwater catchment, dew collection
- `purification/` — boiling, chemical (chlorine/iodine), filtration (ceramic, sand/biosand, UV/SODIS), distillation
- `storage/` — safe containers, cistern design, preventing algae/contamination
- `wells-pumps/` — hand-dug wells, hand pumps, well maintenance

## Recommended sources
- CDC/WHO water treatment guidance (public domain / open)
- Peace Corps well-construction manuals (public domain, US Gov)
- Appropedia water/sanitation articles (CC)
- Biosand filter construction guides (CAWST — open license)
