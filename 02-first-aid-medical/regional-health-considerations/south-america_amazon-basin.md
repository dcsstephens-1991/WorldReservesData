# Amazon Basin — Health Considerations

## Climate-driven risks
- Consistently hot, humid tropical rainforest climate; seasonal river flood pulse affects travel and settlement patterns.
- Dense canopy and remoteness — similar evacuation-time challenges to the Congo Basin file in this archive.

## Disease patterns
- **Malaria** — significant risk in forested areas, particularly away from major cities.
- **Yellow fever** — endemic; vaccination where reachable is the primary defense.
- **Dengue** — widespread.
- **Leishmaniasis** — sandfly-borne, relevant for anyone spending extended time in forest camps.

## Dangerous species
See `06-animals-insects/` for identification detail:
- Bushmaster — the largest venomous pit viper in the Americas, found in this subregion
- Fer-de-lance
- Anaconda (constrictor, not venomous, but capable of serious injury; generally avoids humans)
- Electric eel and piranha present in some waters — real but often overstated hazards; more caution warranted around low-visibility, slow-moving water generally

## Notes
- As with the Congo Basin, remoteness means evacuation can take days — weight prevention (bite avoidance, water treatment, netting) heavily over reactive treatment planning.
