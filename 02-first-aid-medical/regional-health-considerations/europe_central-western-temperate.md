# Central & Western Europe — Health Considerations

## Climate-driven risks
- Temperate, four distinct seasons, generally moderate — one of the lower environmental-risk profiles in this archive.
- Occasional severe summer heatwaves in recent years have caused significant mortality in populations unaccustomed to sustained high heat — don't assume this climate is immune to heat illness risk.

## Disease patterns
- **Tick-borne encephalitis and Lyme disease** — present in forested areas, spring through fall; check for ticks after time in brush/woodland.
- Otherwise a low infectious disease burden relative to most subregions in this archive.

## Dangerous species
See `06-animals-insects/` for identification detail:
- European adder (*Vipera berus*) — the only native venomous snake across much of this subregion; bites are rarely fatal to healthy adults but still require medical attention.
- Ticks are the primary vector-borne concern, not large/venomous wildlife.

## Notes
- Well-developed medical infrastructure generally means faster evacuation and care access here than most other subregions in this archive.
