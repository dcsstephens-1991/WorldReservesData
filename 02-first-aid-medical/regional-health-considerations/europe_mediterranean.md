# Mediterranean Europe — Health Considerations

## Climate-driven risks
- Hot, dry summers bring significant wildfire risk across much of this subregion.
- Mild, wet winters; flash flooding possible in some areas after intense autumn rain events.

## Disease patterns
- **West Nile virus** — some regional transmission recorded in parts of southern Europe.
- **Tick-borne encephalitis** — present in some forested/rural areas.
- Generally low infectious disease burden relative to tropical regions in this archive.

## Dangerous species
See `06-animals-insects/` for identification detail:
- Only mildly venomous native vipers present (e.g. asp viper) — snakebite risk here is low relative to most other subregions in this file set.
- Scorpions present in southern Mediterranean areas, generally mild sting severity.

## Notes
- Wildfire season is the standout regional risk here. Seismic risk is elevated in parts of Italy, Greece, and the Balkans — cross-reference `01-quick-reference/emergency-type-triage.md`.
