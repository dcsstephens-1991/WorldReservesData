# Australian Tropical North & Coast — Health Considerations

## Climate-driven risks
- Tropical monsoon climate; wet season (roughly November–April) brings cyclone risk and flooding.

## Disease patterns
- **Dengue** present in some northern areas.
- **Ross River virus** (mosquito-borne) more broadly across Australia, notable here.

## Dangerous species
See `06-animals-insects/` for identification detail:
- **Saltwater crocodile** — extremely dangerous, present in northern rivers, estuaries, and beaches; heed all local warning signage without exception.
- **Box jellyfish and Irukandji jellyfish** in northern coastal waters, particularly during wet season.
- Also carries the venomous snake/spider risk noted in the Arid Interior & Temperate South file.

## Notes
- Marine sting/bite risk here is among the most severe in this entire archive — check local advisories before any water entry, especially during wet season.
