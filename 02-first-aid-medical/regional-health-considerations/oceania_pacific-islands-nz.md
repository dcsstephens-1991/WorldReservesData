# Pacific Islands & New Zealand — Health Considerations

## Climate-driven risks
- Highly variable by latitude: New Zealand is temperate, most Pacific islands are tropical maritime with cyclone exposure (seasonal, varies by hemisphere/location within the region).

## Disease patterns
- **Dengue and other mosquito-borne disease** present on many tropical islands.
- New Zealand has a notably low infectious disease burden by comparison to the tropical Pacific islands.

## Dangerous species
See `06-animals-insects/` for identification detail:
- New Zealand notably has no native land snakes and very few dangerous land species.
- Tropical Pacific islands vary considerably; some coastal areas carry venomous marine life (cone snails, stonefish) worth checking locally before entering the water.
