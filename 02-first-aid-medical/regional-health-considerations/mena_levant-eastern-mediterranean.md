# Levant & Eastern Mediterranean — Health Considerations

## Climate-driven risks
- Mediterranean coastal climate transitioning to arid desert further inland (Jordanian/Syrian desert).

## Disease patterns
- **Leishmaniasis** (sandfly-borne) present in some areas.
- Otherwise moderate infectious disease burden, generally lower than tropical regions in this archive.

## Dangerous species
See `06-animals-insects/` for identification detail:
- Palestine viper, saw-scaled viper in desert-margin areas

## Notes
- This subregion has experienced significant conflict-related instability in recent decades — cross-reference `01-quick-reference/emergency-type-triage.md` (war/conflict nearby, civil order breakdown) alongside the standard health content here.
