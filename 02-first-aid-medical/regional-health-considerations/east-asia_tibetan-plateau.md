# Tibetan Plateau & East Asian Highlands — Health Considerations

## Climate-driven risks
- Among the most extreme high-altitude environments on Earth outside the Himalaya proper — much of the plateau sits above 4,000m.
- Extreme cold, thin air, and intense UV year-round.

## Disease patterns
- **Altitude sickness** is the dominant medical concern by a wide margin.
- Otherwise low infectious disease burden due to sparse population and a cold climate limiting many disease vectors.

## Dangerous species
See `06-animals-insects/` for identification detail:
- Few dangerous species at this elevation; wolves and occasionally snow leopards present but rarely encountered.

## Notes
- Same ascent discipline as the Himalayan Foothills and Andean Highlands files in this archive — evacuation is slow and highly weather-dependent, so treat altitude illness prevention as the primary planning concern.
