# Northern Europe / Scandinavia — Health Considerations

## Climate-driven risks
- Cold winters, short summers; extreme seasonal day-length variation (near-continuous daylight in summer, near-continuous darkness in winter at high latitude) affects sleep, activity planning, and morale.
- Cold-weather exposure (hypothermia, frostbite) is the dominant environmental risk, especially outside urban areas in winter.

## Disease patterns
- **Tick-borne encephalitis** — present in parts of this subregion, particularly southern Scandinavia and the Baltic states.
- Otherwise a low infectious disease burden.

## Dangerous species
See `06-animals-insects/` for identification detail:
- European adder present in the southern portion of this subregion; otherwise very few dangerous species — among the lowest wildlife-risk profiles in this archive.

## Notes
- Cold-weather survival skills are the dominant concern here, not disease or venomous species — see `02-first-aid-medical/medical-emergencies.md` (hypothermia/frostbite section).
