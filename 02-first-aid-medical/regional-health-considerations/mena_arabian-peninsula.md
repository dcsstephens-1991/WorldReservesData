# Arabian Peninsula — Health Considerations

## Climate-driven risks
- Extreme heat and aridity — among the hottest inhabited regions on Earth. Water scarcity and heat illness prevention are defining, year-round survival factors here, not seasonal concerns.

## Disease patterns
- Relatively low infectious disease burden in developed urban areas.
- Malaria and dengue risk present in parts of Yemen and southern border areas.
- **MERS-CoV** has had regional significance historically, associated with camel contact — avoid close contact with sick camels and unpasteurized camel milk.

## Dangerous species
See `06-animals-insects/` for identification detail:
- Saw-scaled viper, Arabian cobra
- Several medically significant scorpion species

## Notes
- Treat water and shade planning as core survival infrastructure here, similar to the Sahel file in this archive — heat illness is the leading environmental threat by a wide margin.
