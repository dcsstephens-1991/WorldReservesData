# North Africa (Maghreb & Sahara) — Health Considerations

## Climate-driven risks
- Sharp climate split: the Mediterranean coastal strip is temperate, while the Saharan interior is extreme desert with very low precipitation. Plan very differently for a coastal city versus a Saharan crossing.

## Disease patterns
- Relatively low infectious disease burden in coastal/urban areas.
- In the interior, dehydration is the dominant risk, not infectious disease.

## Dangerous species
See `06-animals-insects/` for identification detail:
- Saw-scaled viper, horned viper (desert-adapted, sand-camouflaged, sidewinding movement — watch for its distinctive trail in sand)
- Deathstalker scorpion — one of the most medically significant scorpion species, found across this subregion and into the Middle East

## Notes
- Water discipline in the Saharan interior deserves the same weight given to the Sahel and Arabian Peninsula files in this archive.
