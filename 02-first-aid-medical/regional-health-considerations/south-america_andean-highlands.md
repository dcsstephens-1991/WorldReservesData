# Andean Highlands — Health Considerations

## Climate-driven risks
- High elevation (several major cities — La Paz, Cusco, Quito — sit above 2,800m) brings thin air, intense UV, and large diurnal temperature swings.
- Rapid weather changes at altitude; landslide risk on steep terrain during rainy season.

## Disease patterns
- **Altitude sickness (AMS, and its severe forms HAPE/HACE)** — the dominant medical concern in this subregion; ascend gradually, descend at signs of severe symptoms.
- Lower tropical infectious disease burden than the Amazon Basin at higher elevations, but typhoid/waterborne illness risk remains in rural areas without treated water.

## Dangerous species
See `06-animals-insects/` for identification detail:
- Comparatively few dangerous species at high elevation; pumas present in remote areas but rarely encountered.

## Notes
- Same ascent discipline as the Himalayan Foothills and Tibetan Plateau files in this archive — evacuation from remote high-altitude areas is slow and weather-dependent.
