# Evacuation / Bug-Out Checklist

## Documents & money (waterproof pouch)
- [ ] ID, passports, insurance, deeds/titles (or photos/copies stored on this drive's `14-software-digital/`)
- [ ] Cash in small bills (power/card systems may be down)
- [ ] This drive + a charged phone/reader with Kiwix installed

## Water & food
- [ ] Water: 1 gallon/person/day, 3-day minimum
- [ ] Purification method (bleach drops, filter, tablets) — see `water-purification-quicksteps.md`
- [ ] Food: 3-day minimum, no-cook or easy-cook, high calorie density

## Medical
- [ ] First aid kit + this drive's `02-first-aid-medical/` and this folder's `triage-and-first-aid-card.md`
- [ ] Prescription medications (rotate stock so nothing expires unnoticed)
- [ ] N95/dust masks

## Tools & light
- [ ] Multi-tool, fixed-blade knife
- [ ] Flashlight/headlamp + spare batteries or hand-crank
- [ ] Fire starter (see `fire-shelter-signal.md`)
- [ ] Duct tape, paracord, tarp

## Power & comms
- [ ] Battery bank + solar charger
- [ ] Battery/hand-crank radio
- [ ] Whistle
- [ ] Physical paper map of your area (don't rely on GPS)

## Clothing & shelter
- [ ] Weather-appropriate layers, sturdy shoes
- [ ] Emergency blanket / bivy
- [ ] Rain protection

## Before you leave
- [ ] Know at least 2 routes out of your area
- [ ] Tell someone outside the area where you're going
- [ ] Shut off utilities if instructed/safe to do so (gas especially, if damage suspected)
- [ ] Grab pets' food/leash/carrier

## Decision triggers (fill in for your situation ahead of time)
- What specific conditions mean "leave now, don't wait"?
- Where are you going (primary + backup destination)?
- Who needs to be contacted/gathered before leaving?
