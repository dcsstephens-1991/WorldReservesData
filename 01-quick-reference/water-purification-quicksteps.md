# Water Purification Quick Steps

Untreated water can carry bacteria, viruses, and parasites even if it looks clean. Always treat water from an unknown source.

## Order of operations
1. **Pre-filter** cloudy/dirty water through cloth or let sediment settle, to help disinfection work.
2. **Disinfect** using one of the methods below.
3. **Store** in a clean, covered container.

## Method 1 — Boiling (kills everything, most reliable)
- Bring to a **rolling boil for 1 minute** (3 minutes above 6,500 ft / ~2,000m elevation).
- Let cool before drinking. Improves taste by pouring between containers a few times.

## Method 2 — Household bleach (unscented, no additives, ~5-6% sodium hypochlorite)
- **2 drops per liter / ~8 drops per gallon** of clear water.
- Double the dose if water is cloudy.
- Stir, wait **30 minutes** before drinking. Should have a faint chlorine smell — if not, repeat the dose.

## Method 3 — Iodine (2% tincture)
- **5 drops per liter / ~20 drops per gallon** of clear water (double if cloudy).
- Wait 30 minutes. Not recommended for pregnant people or those with thyroid conditions, or for long-term daily use.

## Method 4 — Filtration
- Ceramic or hollow-fiber filters (rated ≤0.2 micron) remove bacteria/parasites but **not** most viruses — combine with chemical disinfection or boiling if virus risk is likely (e.g. near human sewage).
- Biosand/slow sand filters: effective but need time to build a biological layer (~2-3 weeks) before fully effective.

## Method 5 — Solar disinfection (SODIS) — low-resource backup
- Fill clear plastic bottles with water (relatively clear, <30 NTU turbidity).
- Lay in full direct sun on a reflective surface for **6 hours** (2 consecutive days if cloudy).
- Works via UV + heat; least reliable method, use when nothing else is available.

## Rules of thumb
- When in doubt, boil — it's the only method that reliably kills everything including resistant parasite cysts (e.g. Cryptosporidium) without special filters.
- Never drink floodwater, water near latrines/graves, or stagnant water without treating.
- Rainwater collected cleanly is generally safer than ground sources but still treat if storing long-term.
