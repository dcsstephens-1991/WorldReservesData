## Format for each entry
### [Common name] (*Latin name*)
- **Identifying features**: at least 2 independent distinguishing traits (leaf shape + arrangement, bark, flower structure, smell) — never rely on a single trait or a photo alone.
- **Toxic look-alikes**: name them explicitly, and how to tell them apart.
- **Edible parts & season**: which parts, what time of year.
- **Preparation needed**: raw-safe, or requires cooking/leaching to remove toxins.
- **Source**: which reference this was verified against.

## Rule that overrides everything else
If you cannot positively confirm identification against at least two independent criteria from a trusted source, **do not eat it**. Misidentification of wild plants and fungi kills people every year — this file is a memory aid for trained/verified knowledge, not a substitute for learning to identify local flora before you need to.
