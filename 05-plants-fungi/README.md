# 05 — Plants & Fungi

**Highest-caution category in this archive** — misidentification can kill. Read `docs/CURATION.md`'s safety-critical rules before adding or trusting content here: every edibility claim needs at least two independent identification criteria, named toxic look-alikes, and a source citation.

## Why this folder is more conservative than others in the archive
Most of this archive favors broad, useful coverage. This folder deliberately does not: exhaustive species-level identification guides (especially for fungi) generated from general knowledge carry a real risk of confidently-stated errors that could hurt someone. Where the archive doesn't have a verified, cross-checked source for a specific regional species, it says so rather than guessing — see `fungi-foraging-safety.md` for the clearest example of this policy.

## Recommended sources for expanding this folder
- Regional agricultural extension office foraging/toxic-plant guides (usually free, locally accurate, often public domain)
- Wikipedia species pages (via the Kiwix pack) cross-referenced with a dedicated regional field guide
- Peterson Field Guide series or equivalent regional guides (check licensing — likely reference-only, not redistributable, but useful for cross-checking claims before adding them here)
- Local mycological societies for fungi specifically — see `fungi-foraging-safety.md` on why this is the right source for that category in particular
