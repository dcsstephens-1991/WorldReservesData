# Core Curriculum Outline

A roadmap for re-teaching foundational science and math to a new generation, prioritized by what unlocks the most other knowledge in this archive. Not a textbook itself — see `Still to build` and `docs/PLAN.md` for the plan to add real textbook content (OpenStax and similar open-license sources are the intended source, per `docs/CURATION.md`).

## Why this order
Math underlies essentially everything else (measurement, construction, chemistry ratios, navigation); basic literacy and math come first, followed by the sciences roughly in the order that other archive folders depend on them (chemistry underlies `10-chemistry-materials/` and medicine; physics underlies `08-energy-power/` and `07-shelter-construction/`; biology underlies `04-food-agriculture/`, `05-plants-fungi/`, `06-animals-insects/`, and medicine).

## Suggested progression

### Foundational (all ages, first priority)
- Literacy (reading, writing) — everything else in this archive depends on it.
- Arithmetic — the four basic operations, fractions, decimals, percentages; measurement and units.
- Basic scientific method — observation, hypothesis, testing, revising conclusions based on evidence; this is a thinking tool as much as a content area, and worth teaching early and reinforcing throughout.

### Intermediate
- Pre-algebra and algebra — solving for unknowns, proportional reasoning (directly useful for recipe/mixture scaling, construction, dosing).
- Basic geometry — area, volume, angles (directly useful for construction, land measurement, navigation).
- Basic biology — cells, living systems, ecology, genetics fundamentals (directly useful for agriculture, medicine, animal husbandry).
- Basic chemistry — see `10-chemistry-materials/basic-chemistry-reference.md` for a starting point already in this archive.
- Basic physics — forces, energy, simple machines (directly useful for `07-shelter-construction/structural-basics.md` and `08-energy-power/mechanical-power-basics.md`, both already in this archive).

### Advanced
- Algebra II, geometry/trigonometry in more depth, introductory calculus — needed for more advanced engineering.
- Chemistry and physics in more depth, including the quantitative/lab-work side.
- Advanced biology — microbiology (directly relevant to medicine and food safety), advanced genetics.

## Teaching without a school system
- Multi-age, one-room-schoolhouse-style teaching (older/more advanced students helping teach younger ones) is a well-documented, practical model for education without dedicated age-segregated infrastructure.
- Hands-on application (using the actual skills from other archive folders — measuring a real structure, calculating a real recipe scale-up, observing a real ecosystem) reinforces abstract material far more effectively than lecture alone, and requires no specialized classroom equipment.
